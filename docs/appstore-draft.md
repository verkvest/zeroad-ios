## Description — EN
Bronze Legions: command the great civilizations of antiquity in a full real-time strategy game, rebuilt for touch.

• Gather food, wood, stone and metal, grow your city and advance through the phases
• Train citizen-soldiers, archers, cavalry, champions and heroes
• Skirmish against the computer on hand-crafted and random maps
• Many civilizations, each with its own buildings, units and technologies
• Touch controls made for the phone: tap to select, drag to box-select, long-press for details, two-finger pan and pinch zoom, large buttons
• Universal: iPhone and iPad. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account needed
